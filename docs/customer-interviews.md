# Customer Interviews

Interviews were conducted by Charlie Erickson on November 25, 2026 with three coaches from the University of Calgary baseball program: Luke Senft (hitting coach), Brett Potter (hitting coach), and Ethan Webster (pitching coach). Each coach was asked the same seven questions, including their reaction to CoachIQ. Answers were verified by the interviewer and confirmed by each coach.

Objective: Understand the challenges coaches face when managing teams, tracking player statistics, creating lineups, and planning practices, and evaluate the usefulness of CoachIQ and identify potential improvements.

Participants: 3 coaches

## Interview 1: Luke Senft, Hitting Coach
Interviewer: Charlie Erickson
Interview date: November 25, 2026
Answers verified by interviewer: Yes
Confirmed by coach: Yes, November 25, 2026

1. How do you currently manage your team's statistics and player information?
Mainly Excel spreadsheets and notes during games. It works, but staying organized is time-consuming.

2. What is your biggest challenge when managing your team?
Tracking individual player performance across the season and identifying who needs additional development.

3. What was your first impression of CoachIQ?
Professional and easy to navigate, with everything in one place.

4. Which feature would you find most useful?
Player statistics and performance tracking, with visual representations of performance that make evaluating players easier.

5. Would you use the AI-generated lineup feature?
Yes, as help when deciding a starting lineup, with the coach making the final decision.

6. What improvements would you suggest?
An option to export player statistics and performance reports.

7. Would you consider using this website throughout your season?
Yes, especially if it saves time compared to spreadsheets.

## Interview 2: Brett Potter, Hitting Coach
Interviewer: Charlie Erickson
Interview date: November 25, 2026
Answers verified by interviewer: Yes
Confirmed by coach: Yes, November 25, 2026

1. How do you currently manage your team's statistics and player information?
A combination of phone, handwritten notes, and different applications.

2. What is your biggest challenge when managing your team?
Planning effective practices and determining which areas the team needs to improve on.

3. What was your first impression of CoachIQ?
Liked the layout and the support for multiple sports; seems straightforward to use.

4. Which feature would you find most useful?
AI practice recommendations, with suggestions based on how the team has been performing.

5. Would you use the AI-generated lineup feature?
As a recommendation tool, to consider lineup combinations that might not have been thought of.

6. What improvements would you suggest?
A team calendar where coaches can schedule practices, games, and other team activities.

7. Would you consider using this website throughout your season?
Yes, particularly if it is easy to use on a phone during practices and games.

## Interview 3: Ethan Webster, Pitching Coach
Interviewer: Charlie Erickson
Interview date: November 25, 2026
Answers verified by interviewer: Yes
Confirmed by coach: Yes, November 25, 2026

1. How do you currently manage your team's statistics and player information?
Mostly recorded manually, relying heavily on personal observation when making coaching decisions.

2. What is your biggest challenge when managing your team?
Finding enough time to analyze player performance, prepare lineups, and organize practices.

3. What was your first impression of CoachIQ?
Well organized, with several coaching tools available on one platform, which is appealing.

4. Which feature would you find most useful?
Comparing individual player statistics and seeing how players improve throughout the season.

5. Would you use the AI-generated lineup feature?
Yes, with the ability to manually adjust the suggested lineup for player availability and game situations.

6. What improvements would you suggest?
An attendance tracker and a way for assistant coaches to access the same team information.

7. Would you consider using this website throughout your season?
Yes, provided that entering player statistics is quick and the platform is reasonably priced.

## Key Findings

| Area | Insight |
|------|---------|
| Time management | Coaches want to reduce administrative work compared to spreadsheets and manual notes |
| Player statistics | Visual statistics and player comparisons would simplify evaluation |
| AI-generated lineups | Coaches value recommendations but want final control and the ability to adjust manually |
| Practice planning | AI recommendations could improve practice preparation |
| Accessibility | Mobile use during practices and games is important |
| Requested improvements | Exportable reports, team calendar, attendance tracking, shared assistant coach access |
| Adoption conditions | Time savings, quick data entry, and reasonable pricing |

## Summary
All three coaches said they would consider using CoachIQ throughout their season. Their current methods (spreadsheets, handwritten notes, phone apps, and personal observation) are manual and time-consuming. They see value in combining player statistics, AI lineup suggestions, and practice planning on one platform, as long as coaches keep final control, data entry is quick, and the tool works well on a phone.
