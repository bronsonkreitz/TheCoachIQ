# Market Research

## Target Customer
Volunteer and part-time coaches of youth, school, and community sports teams who currently track players, results, and team information using spreadsheets, paper notes, and group chats.

## Problem Statement
Amateur coaches have no simple, affordable way to keep team and performance data in one place. Their information is scattered across spreadsheets, notebooks, and messaging apps. This makes it hard to spot performance trends, easy to lose or corrupt data, and slow to share information with assistant coaches, players, and parents. Many existing tools are built for leagues, clubs, or professional programs, which makes them more complex and more expensive than an individual coach needs.

## Competitor Analysis

| Competitor | What it does | Gap that CoachIQ addresses |
|------------|--------------|----------------------------|
| TeamSnap | Team scheduling, messaging, and payments | Focused on logistics and communication rather than performance tracking and coaching insights |
| SportsEngine | League, club, and registration management | Designed for organizations, not for an individual coach |
| Hudl | Video analysis and performance tools | Video-centered and aimed at competitive programs, which can be costly for amateur coaches |
| GameChanger | Scorekeeping and stats, mainly for baseball and softball | Limited sport coverage and stats-focused |
| Spreadsheets (Excel, Google Sheets) | Manual tracking and calculations | No guided structure, easy to corrupt, and not a shareable live dashboard |

Note: competitor features and pricing change over time. Details above are general descriptions and should be rechecked against each company's website before final submission.

## Our Differentiation
- Built for the individual amateur coach rather than for leagues or organizations
- Simple setup that requires no technical skills
- Manage multiple teams in one place
- Live dashboards that can be shared with assistants and players
- Built-in data validation that protects against bad or damaged data
- Storage status and warnings so coaches do not silently lose their work
- Demo mode so a new coach can try the app immediately without entering data

## Is the Market Saturated?
The sports software market has many tools for scheduling, registration, and communication, and a few for video analysis. Far fewer products focus on lightweight performance tracking and dashboards for the individual amateur coach. CoachIQ targets that gap by staying simple, affordable, and coach-focused rather than competing as a full league management platform.
