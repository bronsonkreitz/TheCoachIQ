---
name: Saved data recovery
description: Non-destructive handling of damaged or unsupported browser-local work
---

Damaged or unsupported saved sports must remain recoverable until the coach explicitly confirms a reset. Healthy neighboring sports should remain usable and save without erasing the affected entries.

**Why:** Silently substituting demo data and then autosaving destroys the coach's original work. Browser-read failures also do not prove that storage is empty.

**How to apply:** Validate before deriving views, retain rejected entries when saving healthy work, offer a recovery copy before destructive reset, and pause writes when the overall saved snapshot cannot be read safely. A reset of one sport must not reset another.
